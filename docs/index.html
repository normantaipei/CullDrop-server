From memory card to selected photos, on your iPhone or iPad.

Copy a whole card, get on-device star ratings, and cull in offline albums. No laptop and no account.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Copy the card once

JPGs first, then RAW. Each file is verified as it copies, and the app tells you when the card is safe to remove.

Rated on your device

Every photo gets a star rating and tags, with no upload. Burst shots are grouped so you can compare them.

Cull in offline albums

Browse by stars, filter by camera, delete low-rated shots in one step, and save your picks to Photos.

Share with your own server

Optional. Run Culldrop Server yourself for client gallery links, high-rated-first uploads, and NAS backup.

Your photos stay yours

No account, no ads, no tracking. The developer never receives your photos, and they are never used to train AI.

Requires iOS 18 or later. Importing directly from a memory card needs a USB-C or Lightning card reader.
