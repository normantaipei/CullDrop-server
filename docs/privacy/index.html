Culldrop Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Culldrop is designed so your photos stay under your control. The developer does not collect, receive, or have access to your photos or any other data from the app.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No AI training: your photos and data are never used to train or improve any AI model. The models in the app come ready-made and only run on your device.
  • No connections to any server run by the developer.

Data stored on your device

  • Photos you import from a card or folder or add from your photo library, with their ratings and tags. Ratings and tags are generated on your device with Apple's Vision framework.
  • Settings, such as your server URL and camera names. The device token is stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Face data, if you turn on Face Grouping.

Imported JPGs can be included in your own iCloud device backup under your Apple Account settings. RAW files and face data are excluded from that backup.

Face Grouping

Face Grouping is off until you choose to turn it on. When it is on, the app analyzes faces in your photos on your device and stores numeric face data, used only to group photos by person. Face data is never uploaded, sold, or shared, and is not used for advertising or to identify anyone outside the app.

If you create a person link, the name you gave that person and their photo list are sent to the server you set up. Photos may show other people, so get their consent first.

Sync People is off by default. When you turn it on, the people you have named (their names and each person's photo list) are saved to the server you set up, so your other devices on the same server show the same names and groups. Numeric face data still stays on your device and is never uploaded. Turning it off stops sending this information, and deleting face data also clears the synced names from your server.

It stays on your device until you delete it. To delete it, go to Settings → Face Grouping → Delete Face Data. Deleting the app also removes it.

Your own server

If you enter a server in Settings, the app sends that server the photos you upload and related information: file names, capture time, ratings, tags, camera identifiers, credit name, album details, and device name. This server is run by you or someone you choose, not by the developer, and its operator is responsible for that data. Without a server, nothing leaves your device unless you share it yourself.

Anyone with a gallery link can view that album. Gallery pages load no third-party content.

Permissions

  • Local network: to reach a server on your network.
  • Add to Photos: to save photos you choose. The app cannot read your library this way.
  • Photo picker and Files: only the items you select are shared with the app.

Apple

If you agree to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that data.

Deleting your data

Delete photos and albums in the app, or delete the app to remove all data on your device. Data on your own server or NAS must be deleted there. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request from the developer.

Children

Culldrop is a tool for photographers and is not directed at children.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
